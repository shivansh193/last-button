Your old PayPal buttons may stop working.

Paste your website's address. Last Button finds every old PayPal button, rebuilds it as a PayPal Payment Link with the same product, options, postage and tax, and proves each new link takes a payment before you swap it in.

"PayPal may retire WPS anytime after January 1, 2027." PayPal Developer, Website Payments Standard deprecation. Buy Now and Add to Cart buttons are affected; Donate and Subscribe are not.
A pretend 2010 theatre site with 9 old buttons.
  1. Reading your pages (same site only, up to 30, respecting robots.txt)
  2. Finding PayPal buttons, including ones built by JavaScript
  3. Rebuilding each one; AI reads the page for buttons whose settings are hidden
01 / Find

Every button, on every page

Buy Now, Add to Cart, saved and encrypted buttons are flagged. Donate and Subscribe buttons are noticed and left alone, because PayPal says they aren't affected.

02 / Rebuild

Same price, options, postage, tax

Readable settings are copied exactly. For saved or encrypted buttons, Gemini reads the page around them and must quote the words it used. Prices it found wait for your one click.

03 / Prove

A real sandbox payment, checked

A test buyer pays through each new PayPal Payment Link. The order is read back from PayPal and the amount checked against price + postage + tax. Only then does a row turn green.

Made for the people who pasted a button in 2009

Theatres, churches, clubs, craft sellers, old WordPress and static sites. The button code was copied from PayPal years ago and now lives in a CMS nobody wants to touch. Plugins such as Gravity Forms and Timely are already warning their users. Pasted buttons get no warning at all.

You get a patched page to download, a one-line script that swaps the buttons for you, and a spreadsheet of every new link.

Buy Now cmd=_xclickmay stop working
Add to Cart cmd=_cartmay stop working
Saved / encrypted _s-xclickmay stop working
Donate _donationsnot affected
Subscribe _xclick-subscriptionsnot affected

Last Button runs on the PayPal sandbox: links it creates are test links, and the buyer is a test account. It does not touch your live site; you choose when to swap the buttons in.

Built with PayPal Payment Links and Buttons, Orders v2 and webhooks; Gemini; Playwright; AG Grid; Render. MIT licensed. Source on GitHub.
